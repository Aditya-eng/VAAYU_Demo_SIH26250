The fighter GLBs were obtained from the credited public collection at https://airframearchive360.com/ (https://models.airframearchive360.com/dassault_rafale.glb and https://models.airframearchive360.com/sukhoi_su-35_fighter_jet.glb). Each original GLB embeds its title, author, source and CC-BY-4.0 license in asset metadata.

Changes: runtime rotation, centering, scaling, lighting/material adjustments and approximate inspection-zone overlays. The original files are retained. su35-low.glb is a geometry-simplified derivative for software rendering, created using glTF Transform and Meshoptimizer. Embedded textures are used in WebGL; the SVG compatibility renderer uses untextured materials.

The assets do not represent real VAAYU aircraft, operational configurations or performance specifications. All fleet identifiers, maintenance records and capabilities are synthetic. Inspection zones are approximate external reference regions, not manufacturer CAD segmentation or approved maintenance instructions. The separate exploded systems view is an illustrative assembly.
